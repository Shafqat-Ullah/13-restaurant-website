About our Restaurant

Fresh, seasonal & cooked with love

The Restaurant

Tasty began as a small family kitchen and grew into a neighbourhood favourite. Every morning our chefs visit the market, choosing the day's vegetables, fish and meat before the menu is written.

The dining room is relaxed and candlelit, with an open kitchen that fills the air with the smell of garlic, herbs and fresh bread.

Our History

  • Our Experience

  • The Founders Meet

    Two friends who met over a shared love of cooking decided to open a small kitchen with a big heart and an even bigger menu.

  • Create A Restaurant

    We found a corner space, painted the walls warm ochre and built an open kitchen so guests could watch every dish come to life.

  • Added 200+ Employees

    Word spread quickly, and soon our little kitchen grew into a team of cooks, servers and bakers who all feel like family.


  • More Restaurants Outlet

  • Stablished Restaurant in Europe

    Encouraged by our regulars, we carried our recipes across the ocean and opened a second home in Europe.

  • Franchise Restaurants Brooklyn

    Back in the city, a new outpost opened in Brooklyn, bringing our wood-fired oven and late-night pasta to the borough.

  • Added 100K More Employees

    Today our growing team keeps the same promise made on day one - honest food, served with a smile.

  • Stablished Marketing

    We still do things the old way: good food, good wine and a table that is always ready for you.
