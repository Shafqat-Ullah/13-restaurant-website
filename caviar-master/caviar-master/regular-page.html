Inside the Caviar Kitchen

Our approach to seasonal fine dining

Every morning our buyers walk the market stalls before sunrise, hand-selecting the day's fish, vegetables and herbs. Whatever is freshest shapes the evening's specials board, so no two visits to Caviar are ever quite the same. We keep the menu short and honest: a few starters, a few mains, and one dessert that changes with the season.

Cooking is not about making something complicated. It is about making something delicious out of simple, honest ingredients, cooked with patience and served with love.

Elena Moreau, Executive Chef

Our wine cellar holds over three hundred labels, from small biodynamic growers to the great classified châteaux. The sommelier is always happy to guide you, whether you are pairing a single glass with dessert or exploring a full flight. We also offer a daily chef's tasting menu, seven courses designed to be shared slowly across a long, relaxed evening.
